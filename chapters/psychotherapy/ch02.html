The Counselor's Values and the Therapeutic Process

BIG IDEANo counselor is value-free; help clients find answers that fit their own values.
Bracket, don't broadcast.
ValuesOur values are core beliefs that influence how we act, in both our personal and our professional lives.(Corey, 2024, p. 24)
VALUES INFLUENCE
assessmentgoalsinterventionstopics discussedhow progress is evaluatedhow clients' lives are interpreted
Counselor's roleto create a climate in which clients can examine their thoughts, feelings, and actions and to empower them to arrive at their own solutions to problems they face(Corey, 2024, p. 24)
Counselor's taskto assist individuals in finding answers that are most congruent with their own values(Corey, 2024, p. 24)
BracketingManaging your personal values so they do not contaminate the counseling process(Corey, 2024, p. 24)
Value impositioncounselors directly attempting to define a client's values, attitudes, beliefs, and behaviors(Corey, 2024, p. 25)
ACA STANDARD A.4.b · PERSONAL VALUES
Be awareAvoid imposingSeek training where at risk
Jones-Smith: nonverbal imposition (looking away, crossing arms)"values bracketing"
WHEN TO REFER
✓ Referlack of knowledge and skills
✗ Nevervalue disagreement

Value conflict → supervision or consultation. Value exploration is why many programs require personal therapy.

COREY

It is unethical for a counselor to refer a client based on a lack of agreement on values.

DON'T CONFUSE
Bracketingsetting your values aside
Value impositiondefining the client's values (unethical)
Value explorationexamining your own values
