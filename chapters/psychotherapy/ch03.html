BIG IDEAInformed consent is an ongoing process that makes clients active partners, not a signature.
An ongoing process, not a signature.
Informed consentinvolves the right of clients to be informed about their therapy and to make autonomous decisions pertaining to it(Corey, 2024, p. 48)
Ethical + legal requirementfoundation for the working alliance

2. Dimensions of Confidentiality

BIG IDEANo genuine therapy can occur unless clients trust that what they say is private.
Confidentiality · Codeis an ethical concept, and in most states it is the legal duty of therapists not to disclose information about a client(Corey, 2024, p. 49)

Assessmentconsists of evaluating the relevant factors in a client's life to identify themes for further exploration in the counseling process(Corey, 2024, p. 53)

5. Ethical Aspects of Evidence-Based Practice

BIG IDEAUse what research supports, never at the expense of the relationship.
Evidence-based practice (EBP)the integration of the best available research with clinical expertise in the context of patient characteristics, culture, and preferences(APA Presidential Task Force on Evidence-Based Practice, 2006, in Corey, 2024, p. 55)
Never at the expense of the relationship.
Empirically supported treatments (ESTs)Psychological treatments designed for a specific disorder, such as panic disorder, that have been shown to be effective in controlled research.(Jones-Smith, 2021, p. 18)
3 PILLARS (Norcross et al., 2017) · 3 CORNERSTONES

EClinical expertise

✗ With a former clientgenerally unethical; lifelong prohibition can be argued (power differential, transference)
